Welcome to 'Create new equipment' — Step 1

Choose how this equipment should be tracked. You can change this later from equipment settings.

A Create your equipment as a unique equipment
Click here if you want to create equipment as a unique item with its own product number and barcode, which can be followed and managed individually in the calendar function.
B Create your equipment as a multi-equipment
Click here if you want to create your equipment as a so-called multitool, for which there is no need to know the unique product numbers and barcodes — you just need to know if there is a specific number of these items available. Mostly used for equipment that is likely to return to the warehouse after a hand-out.
C Create your equipment as a consumable
Click here if you want to create equipment as a consumable, i.e. goods that are not expected to return to the warehouse after hand-out. Commonly used for petrol, abrasive paper, nails, screws and other consumables.

Click on one of the options above to continue…
